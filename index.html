How to play

Listen
Press Start sound. Each planet rings a note as it crosses the brass meridian line above the sun. Closer orbits ring higher, and since closer planets also move faster, every system plays its own rhythm.
Play
Drag anywhere to throw a planet. The dotted line shows where it will go and the note it will play. Tap empty sky to drop one into a circular orbit.
Choose
Tap a planet to choose it. You can then give it moons, ride along on it, or remove it. Lagrange points and strings follow the chosen planet.
Explore
Systems loads real and historic setups, each with a short story. Tune changes the sound, the gravity, what's drawn over the sky and the camera.
Keys
Space pause · N new · C clear · M mute · T tune · ? help. Mode keys are listed in Tune.
