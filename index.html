Planet mass
Real uses true mass ratios. Heavier planets tug on each other and on the sun, and resonant systems drift into chaos.
Einstein
General relativity turns every orbit a little each lap. Values are the turn per orbit at 1 au. Earth's real turn is 0.04″, so even 3° is about 300,000 times stronger than reality.
Kepler
Each shaded wedge takes the same time, and Kepler's second law says each has the same area. Ticks mark every 30° of the dial, so they rush together near the sun.
View
Rotating turns the camera with the heaviest planet, so it stays at the top and its Lagrange points stand still.
Lagrange
